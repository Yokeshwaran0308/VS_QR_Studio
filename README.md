🚀 VS QR STUDIO

Create. Customize. Connect.

VS QR STUDIO is a modern web-based QR Code Generator that makes it easy to create, customize and download QR codes. Generate standard QR codes or personalized branded QR codes with your own logo and colours.

🌟 Features

- 🔲 Standard QR Generator – Create QR codes for text and URLs.
- 🖼️ Branded QR Generator – Add your own logo to the centre of your QR code.
- 🎨 Custom Colours – Personalize QR foreground and background colours.
- 📐 Multiple Sizes – Choose your preferred QR code size.
- 👁️ Live Preview – View your generated QR code.
- 📥 Download QR – Save QR codes as PNG images.
- 📱 Easy to Use – Simple and user-friendly interface.
